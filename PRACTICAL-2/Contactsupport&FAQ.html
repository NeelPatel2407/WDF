Frequently Asked Questions
Q1. How do I register for courses?
Ans: Go to the Course Registration page and select your subjects.
Q2. How can I check my attendance?
Ans: Open the Dashboard page to view your attendance.
Q3. How do I pay my fees?
Ans: Open the Fees Payment page and click on "Pay Now".
Q4. I forgot my password. What should I do?
Ans: Click on "Forgot Password" on the Login page and reset your password.
Q5. How can I update my profile?
Ans: Open the Profile page and click the Save button after editing.
